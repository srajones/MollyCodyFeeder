🏠 Home Assistant setup

This adds Poor Puppy sensors to Home Assistant (last meal, today's meals, next meal, hopper…), refreshed automatically. You copy two snippets into HA's config files. About 5 minutes, no coding.
Your existing Home Assistant notifications (the webhook) keep working; this is extra.

Troubleshooting

    
    
      
  • Sensors “Unavailable”: check the HA log (Settings → System → Logs, search rest).
        
      
          
    • 401: wrong username/password in secrets.yaml (quotes around the password, no extra spaces).
      • 
          
    • 429: refreshing too often; keep it at 1 minute or slower.
      • 
          
    • Timeout / SSL error: open  in a browser on the same network as HA; it should ask for your login and then show text.
      • 
        
    • 
      
  • “Invalid config” on restart: almost always indentation. Every line must keep the spaces exactly as copied; YAML doesn't allow tabs.
    • 
      
  • A value is empty (“Unknown”): normal when there's no data yet, e.g. the hopper estimate before you tap “I refilled the hopper”,
        or Today before the first meal of the day.
    • 
      
  • Changed your page password? Update it in secrets.yaml and restart HA.
